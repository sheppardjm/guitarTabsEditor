THE SCIENTIST (ACOUSTIC) - COLDPLAY

An incredibly simple but fantastic song to play on an acoustic guitar!
There seems to be a lot of confusion about the key of the song.
It does state below that a capo is required, but for those that don't have a capo,
I have taken the liberty of putting a set of chords on which do not require a capo
and will allow you to play along with the CD.

EDIT (08/05/2020) - it's been a long time since I originally submitted this and I must say it will NOT let
you play along to the song.  The tuning of the song is very unique.  I think it's tuned in 4ths
(which basically means the high b and e strings become c and f.  These strings are then played as open
throughout).  But even then it still doesn't quite sound right.

Oh well, I'm happy with my version as an acoustic...
