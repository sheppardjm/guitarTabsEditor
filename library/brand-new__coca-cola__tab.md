the demo. I doubt many will come to the same conclusion but that's 
how music works.

The noise in the background here can be recreated by holding a note at the
9th fret on the G string. Also the higher pitched noises later on can be 
recreated using a glass slide placed ALL THE WAY up the strings (like over
the pickups even) and slowly moving it down. I'm pretty sure most of the 
noises are just synth pads but its fun to attempt them.
